ABOUT GIRL GENIUS FOUNDATION

Creating a world where every girl has the opportunity to thrive.

We believe that a girl's future should not be limited by the opportunities available to her today. Our work is driven by the commitment to create pathways for education, empowerment and growth.

WHY WE EXIST

Our Story

Every girl deserves the chance to learn, discover her potential and build a future on her own terms. Yet access to education, mentorship and opportunities for personal development remains uneven.

Girl Genius Foundation exists to help address these challenges by supporting initiatives that empower girls and women with the resources, knowledge and opportunities to move forward.

We believe meaningful change happens when girls are not only given opportunities but are also equipped to make the most of them.

Our Mission

To advance girls' education and women's empowerment by creating access to opportunities, mentorship, skills development and advocacy.

Our Vision

A society where every girl and woman has the opportunity, confidence and resources to reach her full potential and contribute meaningfully to her community.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Core Values

Education

We believe knowledge and access to learning can open doors to new possibilities.

Empowerment

We encourage girls and women to recognize their potential and take ownership of their futures.

Community

We believe lasting progress comes from collaboration and shared responsibility.

Growth

We value continuous learning, development and the courage to explore new possibilities.

Advocacy

We support conversations and actions that promote equal opportunities and positive change.
